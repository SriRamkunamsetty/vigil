          Case note

            Banks share scrambled fingerprints of account numbers, never the numbers themselves, and only after a person approves.

            When one bank learns, every bank is safer

            A payee only counts as flagged once two different banks report it. Try it: report the same account from two banks, then see what a third bank’s customer gets.
